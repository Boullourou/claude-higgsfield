# Higgsfield Talking Head Video Director

## Avatar Selection Guide

**Expert / Authority** — educational, thought leadership, B2B. Measured, confident, clear.
**Relatable Creator** — UGC-adjacent, personal story, social proof. First-person, casual, contractions.
**Brand Character / Soul ID** — brand campaigns, AI influencer, series continuity.
**Neutral Presenter** — product demos, announcements. Clear and benefit-focused.

Platform fit: TikTok → Relatable Creator | Reels → Relatable/Brand | YouTube Shorts → Expert/Neutral | LinkedIn → Expert | Website → Expert/Neutral

## Script Architecture (4 parts)

1. **Open (0–5s)** — Start with viewer's world. Problem-first / Bold claim / Right question. NEVER: "Hey guys..." / "Today I want to talk about..."
2. **Body** — One idea per sentence. Max 2 ideas per paragraph. Structure: Problem→Cause→Solution→Proof (explainer) / Scene→Tension→Turn→Takeaway (story)
3. **Transition to CTA (last 5–8s body)** — Consequence bridge
4. **CTA (final 5s)** — One action. Directional verb. Specific destination.

## Teleprompter Formatting Rules

- Max 15 words per sentence
- Delivery markers: [PAUSE] [EMPHASIS] [SLOWER] [FASTER]
- Paragraph breaks = breath breaks (2–3 sentences max)
- Write out all numbers: $47 → "forty-seven dollars", 3x → "three times"
- Avoid: parenthetical asides, em dashes, ellipses

## Platform Delivery

| Platform | Duration | Energy | Pacing |
|----------|----------|--------|--------|
| TikTok | 15–45s | High | Fast |
| Reels | 15–60s | Medium-high | Moderate |
| YouTube Shorts | 30–60s | Medium | Slightly slower |
| LinkedIn | 30–90s | Low-medium | Measured |
| Website | 60–120s | Low-medium | Deliberate |

## Output: Talking Head Brief

Platform / Avatar / Duration / Framing / Background / Lighting / Energy level / Script (with delivery markers) / On-screen text overlays / Scene direction / CTA / Variation A (shorter) / Variation B (different archetype)
